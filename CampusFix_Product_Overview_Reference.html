THE PLATFORM

Less chasing.
More clarity.

CampusFix brings everyday campus maintenance requests into one organised process, helping students and campus teams stay aligned from report to resolution.

01

Structured reporting

Capture the location, category, description, and optional photo evidence in one request.

02

Clear responsibility

Give administrators a consistent way to review requests and assign the appropriate team.

03

Progress visibility

Keep status changes, work notes, and completion details connected to the original request.

04

Campus-wide communication

Share announcements and important operational information through dedicated workspaces.

HOW CAMPUSFIX WORKS

A straightforward process,
from start to finish.

STEP 01

Submit

A student records the issue, selects its location, and provides useful details.

STEP 02

Review & assign

Administration reviews the request and assigns responsibility to a worker or team.

STEP 03

Work & update

The assigned worker records progress and updates the request as work continues.

STEP 04

Close the loop

The outcome is recorded so the student and relevant teams can refer back to it.
